# Especificaciones de los recursos generados

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada, sin API externa ni clave del usuario.

## cities.png

Fantasy browser RPG environment atlas, wide landscape precisely divided in two equal vertical halves. Left: orcish desert capital inspired by Orgrimmar, red sandstone canyon, spiked timber gates, red banners, orange sunset, travelers. Right: human medieval capital inspired by Stormwind, white towers, blue and gold banners, river, bridge, green hills. Rich hand-painted stylized fantasy art, elevated perspective, dramatic architecture, readable foreground plaza. No text, logos, UI or watermark.

## sprites.png

Production fantasy RPG sprite atlas, exactly four columns and three rows, transparent background, equally sized cells, centered isolated assets with padding. Row 1: orc warrior, human knight, troll shaman, elf ranger. Row 2: undead mage, dwarf hunter, battle axe, breastplate. Row 3: fire staff, horned helmet, wolf enemy, stone guardian. Cohesive painterly fantasy art, readable at small sizes. No text or grid lines. Original static illustrations inspired by Warcraft aesthetics.
